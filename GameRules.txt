Create a complete multiplayer online trick-taking card game inspired by Mendicot using Godot 4.x with ENet networking. The game must support Android, Web, and Desktop. The implementation must be scalable, server-authoritative, perspective-correct for every player, optimized for mobile devices, and built in a modular way for future expansion.

========================================
CORE GAME OVERVIEW
==================

The game is a multiplayer team-based trick-taking card game with hidden/open trump mechanics, dealer selection, dealer rotation, court wins, score accumulation, spectators, reconnect support, bots, and multiple player-count modes.

The game must support:

* 4 players
* 6 players
* 8 players

The host must choose the room format before creating or starting the room:

* player count: 4, 6, or 8
* gameplay direction: counter-clockwise by default, clockwise optional
* target score: default 15, configurable
* private room code or public matchmaking
* bots enabled/disabled
* spectators enabled/disabled
* touch interaction mode defaults
* card/table theme defaults

Default gameplay direction:

* counter-clockwise

The game must be built so 4-player works first for V1, but the rules and architecture must not be hardcoded only for 4 players. The design must already support 6-player and 8-player variants.

========================================
PLAYER COUNTS, DECKS, AND CARD COUNTS
=====================================

4-player mode:

* use full 52-card deck
* no jokers
* each player receives 13 cards
* deal pattern: 5 + 4 + 4

6-player mode:

* use 48-card deck
* remove all four 2s from the deck
* no jokers
* each player receives 8 cards
* deal pattern: 4 + 4

8-player mode:

* use 48-card deck
* remove all four 2s from the deck
* no jokers
* each player receives 6 cards
* deal pattern: 3 + 3

Deck creation must depend on selected player count:

* 4 players = 52 cards
* 6 players = 48 cards, remove 2s
* 8 players = 48 cards, remove 2s

========================================
CARD RANKING
============

Card rank order from highest to lowest:

A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

For 6-player and 8-player modes, 2s are removed, so lowest card is 3.

This ranking applies to:

* dealer selection draw
* trick winner comparison
* sorting
* bot decision logic

========================================
TEAM STRUCTURE
==============

Teams are based on alternating seats.

For 4 players:

* Team A: seat_0 + seat_2
* Team B: seat_1 + seat_3

For 6 players:

* Team A: seat_0 + seat_2 + seat_4
* Team B: seat_1 + seat_3 + seat_5

For 8 players:

* Team A: seat_0 + seat_2 + seat_4 + seat_6
* Team B: seat_1 + seat_3 + seat_5 + seat_7

Team logic must be calculated from absolute seat IDs, not local visual names.

========================================
SEAT SYSTEM AND PLAYER PERSPECTIVE
==================================

The game must use absolute seat IDs internally:

* 4 players: seat_0 to seat_3
* 6 players: seat_0 to seat_5
* 8 players: seat_0 to seat_7

Clients must render the table using local visual perspective.

Every player must see themselves at the bottom of the screen.

For 4 players, local view labels can be:

* my
* right
* top
* left

For 6 and 8 players, visual positions must expand around the table:

* bottom/local player
* lower-right
* right/top-right
* top
* top-left
* left/lower-left
  depending on player count

Absolute seats must be remapped to local visual slots on each client.

Example for 4-player:
If local player is seat_2:

* seat_2 renders as local bottom player
* seat_3 renders to the next visual position based on selected play direction
* seat_0 and seat_1 render relative to that perspective

All gameplay state must use absolute seat IDs:

* dealer
* trump holder
* current turn
* lead player
* played cards
* trick winner
* disconnected player
* spectator view
* bot seat
* score ownership

The client must only convert absolute seat IDs to visual positions for display.

Never use local names like “my”, “right”, “top”, or “left” as authoritative gameplay seats.

========================================
GAMEPLAY DIRECTION
==================

The host can choose play direction before the match:

Direction affects:

Default for V1:

========================================
FIRST GAME DEALER SELECTION
===========================

Before the first game of a match, dealer must be decided using a shared dealer-selection draw.

This dealer-selection phase happens only for the first game in a match.

Dealer draw rules:

* server creates one shared set of random cards equal to player count
* 4-player mode: 4 dealer-draw cards
* 6-player mode: 6 dealer-draw cards
* 8-player mode: 8 dealer-draw cards
* all dealer-draw cards must have different ranks
* these cards are not taken from the actual match deck
* they exist only to decide dealer
* no two dealer-draw cards may share the same rank

Dealer draw selection:

* cards appear face-down in the center of the table
* all players may choose one remaining card
* first valid server-received claim gets the card
* once selected, that card becomes unavailable and unclickable for everyone
* the selected card animates to the choosing player’s reveal/play area
* then it flips face-up publicly
* every player sees the animation from their own perspective
* claimed cards must not reanimate every time another player selects a card
* already claimed cards must stay fixed in their revealed location

After all players have chosen:

* player with highest card becomes dealer

Dealer draw ranking:
A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

Since dealer-draw ranks are unique, no tie is possible.

========================================
DEALER AND TRUMP HOLDER
=======================

Dealer:

* shuffles
* distributes cards

The game/server handles all fairness and rule enforcement.

Trump holder:

* the first served player becomes trump chooser
* this is the player immediately after dealer according to selected play direction
* if direction is counter-clockwise, use counter-clockwise next player
* if direction is clockwise, use clockwise next player

Trump holder receives the first batch and then chooses trump mode:

* Closed Trump
* Open Trump

========================================
DEALING PATTERNS
================

4-player mode:

* deal 5 cards to each player first
* trump holder chooses trump mode and, if closed mode, chooses one card from first 5 as hidden trump
* then deal 4 cards to each player
* then deal final 4 cards to each player
* total: 13 cards each

6-player mode:

* deal 4 cards to each player first
* trump holder chooses trump mode and, if closed mode, chooses one card from first 4 as hidden trump
* then deal 4 more cards to each player
* total: 8 cards each

8-player mode:

* deal 3 cards to each player first
* trump holder chooses trump mode and, if closed mode, chooses one card from first 3 as hidden trump
* then deal 3 more cards to each player
* total: 6 cards each

Important:

* the deal must pause after first batch so trump holder can choose open/closed trump
* if closed trump is chosen, trump holder selects one card from first batch to hide
* while trump holder is choosing, other players see a waiting message
* only trump holder can interact with the trump selection UI
* after selection, dealing continues

========================================
TRUMP MODES
===========

There are two trump modes:

* Closed Trump
* Open Trump

The trump holder chooses which mode to use after receiving the first batch.

========================================
CLOSED TRUMP MODE
=================

Closed Trump flow:

* trump holder selects one card from first dealt batch
* that card is hidden/set aside
* the hidden card is removed from active hand temporarily
* the hidden card is not visible to anyone, including trump holder, after selection
* it remains face-down in a hidden trump area
* game continues with no active trump suit until hidden trump is revealed

Who may reveal:

* any player who cannot follow suit may reveal hidden trump
* the player must be on their turn
* revealing is optional, not compulsory

Reveal behavior:

* hidden trump card flips/reveals publicly for 3 seconds
* all players see the revealed suit
* trump suit becomes active permanently for the rest of the game
* trump icon/logo appears for everyone
* hidden trump card then returns to the original trump holder’s hand
* if the revealing player is forced to play after reveal, legal play must account for the newly returned trump card if it belongs to that player
* after reveal, trump remains active until game ends

Closed trump important behavior:

* player cannot reveal if they can follow lead suit
* player may choose not to reveal and instead discard any legal non-suit card
* reveal must be synchronized for all players
* play button/input must be disabled during reveal animation
* timer should pause or reset during trump reveal

========================================
OPEN TRUMP MODE
===============

Open Trump does NOT mean a card is selected and shown at the start.

Open Trump rules:

* no card is hidden
* game starts with no active trump suit
* no trump card is set aside
* all cards remain in players’ hands
* first time any player cannot follow lead suit and plays a non-lead-suit card, that played card’s suit becomes trump
* trump becomes active from that moment for the rest of the game
* trump icon/logo appears for everyone
* after trump is activated, highest trump wins future tricks

Example:

* lead suit is hearts
* player has no hearts
* player plays spades
* spades becomes trump for the rest of the game

If a player cannot follow suit but plays a non-lead-suit card in open trump mode, that suit becomes trump automatically.

========================================
FOLLOW SUIT RULE
================

Players must follow lead suit if possible.

If player has lead suit:

* player must play a card of lead suit

If player does not have lead suit:

* player may play any card
* in closed trump mode, player may optionally reveal hidden trump before playing
* in open trump mode, the first non-lead-suit card played activates trump

A player may never ignore lead suit if they have it.

========================================
TRICK WINNER LOGIC
==================

Each trick consists of one card played by every active player.

4-player trick:

* 4 cards

6-player trick:

* 6 cards

8-player trick:

* 8 cards

Winner logic:

1. If trump is active and one or more trump cards were played:

   * highest trump wins
2. Otherwise:

   * highest card of lead suit wins

Winner of trick leads next trick.

========================================
GAME END CONDITION
==================

A game/round ends when all cards have been played.

Then calculate:

* number of 10s captured by each team
* number of tricks captured by each team

Court:

* if one team captures all four 10s
* court is awarded regardless of total tricks won

Normal win:

* if no team captured all four 10s
* team with more 10s wins

Tie:

* if teams captured equal number of 10s
* team with more tricks won wins

Score:

* Normal win = 2 points
* Court = 5 points

Match ends when a team reaches target score.

Default target score:

* 15 points

Host may configure:

* 12
* 15
* 21
* custom later

========================================
DEALER ROTATION AFTER EACH GAME
===============================

Dealer rotation depends on:

* current dealer’s team
* winning team
* whether win was normal or court
* play direction

Rules:
If dealer’s team wins normal:

* dealer moves to next player in play direction

If dealer’s team loses normal:

* same dealer deals again

If dealer’s team wins court:

* dealer moves to next player in play direction

If dealer’s team loses court:

* skip next player in play direction
* player after next becomes dealer

This must work for 4, 6, and 8 players.

* do not auto-sort after trump reveal unless user setting allows it

Default:

* user chooses when to sort
